1Who we are & definitions

Controller: NumberInbox — privacy@numberinbox.com.

  • Service – NumberInbox, an email client where your phone number is your identity. Emails sent to +{country code}{phone number}@numberinbox.com are delivered to your inbox.
  • Personal data – any information relating to an identified or identifiable person.
  • User Content – content you create, upload, or send through the Service.

2Scope

This policy covers the NumberInbox app and this static privacy site only. It does not cover your email provider — see §5.

3What we collect

We split this clearly to avoid confusion:

SourceDataPurpose
App useNo account, no analytics SDK, no personal data collected by usProvide the client locally on your device

If we ever add optional website statistics, they will be consent-based only, and you can withdraw consent at any time via your browser settings or by contacting us.

4What we never do

  • No sale, rental, or trade of personal data. No ads. No cross-app profiling.
  • No access to your mailbox content — your credentials stay on your device and connect directly to your provider.
  • No collection of contacts, photos, or files except when you explicitly choose a file to attach.

5Your emails stay with your provider

NumberInbox is a client usable with any email server. Your messages, credentials, and mailbox are stored by the email server owner you configure, under their privacy policy — not ours.

Please review your provider's policy. We cannot be held responsible for the actions of email server owners you choose to use.

6Push notifications

If your provider supports push, new-mail signals may transit via Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google). No email content or personal data is sent to us in this process — FCM carries a minimal wake-up signal, and the app then fetches the actual message directly from your mail server.

7App permissions

All permissions are optional. Denying them only reduces functionality:

Files, documents & audio

To attach a file you pick to an email. Only chosen files are uploaded — to your mail server, never to us.

Photos & videos

To attach a photo/video you pick. Same as above — your choice, your server.

Contacts

For on-device address auto-complete. Your address book never leaves your device, except the single address you explicitly send an email to (which your mail server then sees).

8Legal basis (GDPR Art. 6)

  • Support replies: consent / legitimate interest in answering you.
  • App functionality: performance of the service you requested; permissions based on your consent.
  • Optional statistics (site only, if ever enabled): consent.

9Retention

We store essentially nothing. Support emails are kept only as long as needed to resolve your request, then deleted on request. Anything stored by your mail provider or FCM is governed by their retention schedules.

10Sharing & third parties

  • Your chosen email provider — receives what the email protocol requires to deliver mail.
  • Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging) — as a processor for push delivery, where applicable.
  • We do not share data with advertisers, brokers, or analytics vendors. Disclosure only if legally required.

No international transfer is performed by us. Transfers (if any) happen between your device, your provider, and Google under their terms.

11Your rights

Under GDPR (and applicable local laws) you have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection, and withdrawal of consent.

To exercise them, email privacy@numberinbox.com. We reply within 30 days. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority. Since the app holds no account data about you, most requests will concern support correspondence only — mailbox data must be requested from your provider.

12Children

The Service is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect their data. Contact us if you believe a child provided data and we will delete it.

13Security

We apply data minimisation by design (collect nothing), TLS to your provider, and least-privilege permissions. No method is 100% secure — protect your device and credentials, and use a reputable mail provider.

14Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect app or legal changes. Material changes will be announced via the app/store listing and the “Last updated” date above will be revised. Continued use after the effective date constitutes acceptance.

15 · Contact

Questions about this policy or your rights? Email privacy@numberinbox.com. Please include “Privacy Request” in the subject.
